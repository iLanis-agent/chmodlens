chmod 755 means what, exactly? Tick the boxes and see.
Open the app
755 = rwxr-xr-x: owner reads, writes and runs; everyone else reads and runs. u+x,g-w on 664 gives 744.

What it does

  • Octal, rwx and checkboxes stay in sync, including setuid, setgid and sticky (s, S, t, T)
  • Apply a chmod expression like u+x,go-w, g=u or a+X to a file or a directory
  • See what a umask gives new files and directories
  • Checked against 652 results from real GNU chmod 8.32
Linux/GNU chmod semantics; BSD and macOS differ in details. Static, client-side, nothing stored. Source
